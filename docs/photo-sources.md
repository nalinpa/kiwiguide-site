# Photo sources

Every photo on the site, where it came from and what its licence allows. Any new photo needs the owner's OK first, a licence that allows it, a row here and a credit on the page.

The owner OK'd photos from their `Pictures/southisland` folder on 1 Nov 2026, open licences only: Pexels, CC0, public domain, and Wikimedia CC BY / CC BY-SA. No regional tourism library photos (Great South, CrowdRiff, Brandkit and the like): their terms tie use to promoting the region, unpaid media or non-commercial use. Licences and credits below are as recorded in the app repo's `docs/photo-sources.md`, which holds each file page. A CC BY-SA photo we resize or crop stays CC BY-SA.

On 23 Nov 2026 the owner OK'd the app's own place photos for the trip quiz's "What are you into?" swipe cards (`plan/quiz.js`, `DECK`), regional library photos included: the eleven rows marked "Quiz card" below. They load from the app's image host (`api.blacksands.app/images/southislandguide/`), and each card shows its credit. The open-licence rule above still holds for the rest of the site.
