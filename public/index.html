Kiwi Guide

Plan your South Island road trip

Kiwi Guide is an iPhone app for exploring New Zealand's South Island by road. Pick a suggested trip or build your own, day by day.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone. Free at launch.

Everything for the drive, in one app

Find places, plan the days, and know what you're in for before you get there.

Places, town by town

Walks, lookouts, swims, adventures, and places to eat and stay. Browse a town or find them on the map.

A page for every place

Photos, what to expect, directions and reviews from other travellers.

Suggested trips

From a day in Christchurch to three weeks around the island, starting from Christchurch, Queenstown or Picton.

A day-by-day trip planner

Add places to your trip and see each day's stops and how long you'll be driving.

A travel guide with local knowledge

Driving, weather, sandflies, booking ahead and staying safe, plus a guide to every region.

A photo share card

Put your own photo on a card with the place's name and the date, ready to post or send.

A look inside

From the map to your trip, day by day.

Suggested trips

Pick from 26 ready-made trips, including South Island in 7, 10, 14 and 21 Days from Christchurch, Queenstown or Picton. Copy one into your own trip, then make it yours.

What a day looks like

Day 2 of Christchurch to Queenstown via Tekapo and Mount Cook. Every stop is timed around opening hours and the drive between.

  1. 9:00 am Church of the Good Shepherd
  2. 10:00 am Mt John Summit and Astro Café
  3. 12:30 pm Peters Lookout
  4. 2:00 pm Kea Point Track
  5. 5:30 pm Tasman Glacier View and Blue Lakes
