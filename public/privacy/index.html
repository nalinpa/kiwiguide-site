Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Black Sands Digital ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Kiwi Guide mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and a unique user identifier. You may also sign in with Apple or Google, in which case we receive the identifier and email address those services provide.
  • Location Data: If you allow it, Kiwi Guide uses your location while the app is open to start you in the nearest town and show how far away places are. It's optional, and the app works without it. Your location is used on your phone and isn't sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Photos: If you choose to create a share card, you may select a photo from your camera or photo library. This photo is used only to generate the image you choose to share and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Reviews: Ratings and text reviews you submit for a location are stored against your account and are visible to other users of the app.
  • Usage Data & Crash Reports: We collect anonymous diagnostic data to help us identify bugs and improve the app's performance.
  • Website and app usage (Google Analytics): Our website (kiwiguide.app), including the trip quiz, uses Google Analytics. From version 1.5 the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. They record which pages and steps are used (for example, which quiz questions are answered, the answers picked from the list, such as the start town, trip length, pace and driving time, and whether a trip is saved), the type of device, browser and language, and an approximate location (country and region) worked out from your IP address by Google. They do not record your name or email address, and we do not collect your device's advertising ID.
  • Trips saved from the website: If you save a trip from the quiz, we store your email address (or your Google account's identifier and email, if you sign in with Google) and the trip, the same as if you had created them in the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to provide and improve the app's core functionality and to contact you as described below. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal location history or account details with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

We email you about your account. We'll only send feedback requests or news if you've agreed to it, and every email lets you stop.

We use website and app usage data to see what works, to improve Kiwi Guide, and to measure how well our advertising performs. This is aggregate reporting. We don't use it to identify you or to build advertising profiles.

3. Third-Party Services

Kiwi Guide uses Google Firebase to sign you in securely and to store your account, saved places and trips. Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google are used only to sign you in. Google Analytics (and, from version 1.5, Google Analytics for Firebase) is provided by Google to measure usage. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Cookies and Opting Out

Outside the European Union, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein, the website sets Google Analytics cookies (such as _ga) to tell visits apart. In those regions the website stores no analytics or advertising cookies and only sends Google measurement pings without cookies. You can opt out of Google Analytics on the web with Google's browser add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout), or by blocking cookies in your browser.

5. Data Storage & Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete your account and associated data at any time directly via the settings menu inside the Kiwi Guide application. Upon requesting deletion, your data is permanently removed from our active database.

Accounts made on the website are Kiwi Guide accounts. You can sign in to the app with the same email or Google account, and delete the account in the app under Profile.

6. Children's Privacy

Kiwi Guide does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you are under 13, please do not attempt to register for the app or send any personal information about yourself to us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes, including if paid features are introduced. The date above shows when it was last revised.

Questions about this privacy policy or the Kiwi Guide app? Email info@blacksandsdigital.nz.
