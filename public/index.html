Everything for the drive, in one app

Find places, plan the days, and know what you're in for before you get there.

  • Places, town by town

    Walks, lookouts, swims, adventures, and places to eat and stay. Browse a town or find them on the map.

  • A page for every place

    Photos, what to expect and directions, plus a place to leave your own review.

  • Suggested trips

    From a day in Christchurch to three weeks around the island, starting from Christchurch, Queenstown or Picton.

  • A day-by-day trip planner

    Add places to your trip and see each day's stops, the time for each one and how long you'll be driving.

  • Add the next stop

    After you add a place, see nearby places that fit in that day, and add the next one with a tap.

  • Your trip, marked

    Places in your trip are marked in Explore, in Saved Places and on the map.

  • A travel guide with local knowledge

    Driving, weather, sandflies, booking ahead and staying safe, plus a guide to every region.

  • A photo share card

    Put your own photo on a card with the place's name and the date, ready to post or send.

  • Made for iPad too

    Layouts made for the bigger screen, on iPad and on iPhone Duo.

  • Sign in with Apple or Google

    One tap to sign in with your Apple or Google account, or use your email.

Why not just a map app?

A map app gets you from A to B. Kiwi Guide is built for the days in between.

  • Works offline

    Download the maps before you go and Kiwi Guide works completely offline. On South Island roads the signal will drop.

  • A trip builder with real times

    Each day's stops are timed around opening hours and the drive between, so the plan is one you can keep.

  • Made for the South Island

    Over 500 places in about 40 towns and 26 suggested trips, all for one road trip.

  • Local knowledge

    One-lane bridges, sandflies and what to book ahead: the things a map won't tell you.
