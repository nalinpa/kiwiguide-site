Kiwi Guide

Plan your South Island road trip

Kiwi Guide is a free iPhone app for exploring New Zealand's South Island by road. Pick a suggested trip or build your own, day by day.

Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

What's in the app

  • Over 500 places in about 40 towns

    Walks, lookouts, swims, adventures, and places to eat and stay. Browse them town by town or find them on the map.

  • A page for every place

    Photos, what to expect, directions and reviews from other travellers.

  • 26 suggested trips

    Ready-made trips from Christchurch, Queenstown or Picton, from a day in the city to three weeks around the island.

  • A day-by-day trip planner

    Add places to your trip and see each day's stops and how long you'll be driving.

  • A travel guide with local knowledge

    Driving, weather, sandflies, booking ahead and staying safe, plus a guide to every region.

  • A photo share card

    Put your own photo on a card with the place's name and the date, ready to post or send.

A look inside

Suggested trips

Not sure where to start? There are 26 suggested trips, from Christchurch in a Day to three weeks around the island. South Island in 7, 10, 14 and 21 Days each leave from Christchurch, Queenstown or Picton.

Copy one into your own trip, then add, move or drop places as you go.

A travel guide with local knowledge

The things that catch visitors out, in plain words: one-lane bridges, sandflies, why a drive takes longer than the map says, and what to book before you arrive.
